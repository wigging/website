Hello, I'm a research scientist at the Oak Ridge National Laboratory involved in various modeling, simulation, and programming activities. I currently work on remote instrument control, machine learning, and large language model applications. Previous areas of research include fluidized bed reactors, battery cells and battery packs, rotor dynamics, water treatment, and biomass pyrolysis. I am also creator of the Numerix Swift package, Un1ts macOS app, Chemics Python package, BioComp web app, and the Genja static website generator. I am the former organizer of the KnoxPy Python users group and the Knoxville CocoaHeads group for iOS and macOS developers.
